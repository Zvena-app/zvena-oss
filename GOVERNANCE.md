# Governance

How decisions about zvena are made today. This is a description of the current state, kept short on purpose; it will change when the nonprofit that will operate zvena is registered.

## Who runs zvena

- zvena is a non-commercial project founded and led by **Ethan Merker**, who builds and runs it with a team of five people.
- A nonprofit organization to operate zvena is being registered. It does not exist yet; until it does, the founder is responsible for the zvena.app service.
- There are no investors and no company behind zvena.

## How decisions are made

- **The founder is the final decision-maker** for the product, the code, releases, licensing and money. Day-to-day decisions are delegated to the people working on each area.
- **Proposals.** Anything large — a new section, a new external service or AI provider, a change to what data is collected or where it goes, licensing, pricing — is written up first (an issue or a short document) and decided by the founder after the team has had a chance to comment. The decision and its reason are recorded with the proposal.
- **Code.** Changes are reviewed before they ship; security-sensitive changes get a separate security review.
- **Public statements** (zvena.app, this repository's documents) must match what the code and the data handling actually do. A mismatch is treated as a bug.

## Continuity

If the founder can no longer lead the project, there is no written succession process yet. Writing one — together with the nonprofit's bylaws: board, how maintainers are added or removed, how decisions are taken by more than one person — is an open task (see below).

## Money

zvena has no income today: no paid plans, no ads, no investors. The founder, Ethan Merker, pays all of zvena's running costs personally (servers, storage and backups, email delivery, domains, the model requests behind smart suggestions). If the project starts accepting donations or sponsorship, the sources and how the money is spent will be published, and sponsors get no say over users' data.
